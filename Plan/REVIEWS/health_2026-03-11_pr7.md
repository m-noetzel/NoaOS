## Risks You Are Taking
1. **No CI/CD pipeline (high impact, high likelihood).** Every quality check is manual. A merge without running ruff/mypy/pytest could introduce regressions silently. This is the project's single biggest operational gap.
2. **Two high-severity findings remain open (medium impact).** BE-H4 (SSE replay cursor) means reconnecting SSE clients may miss or duplicate events. BE-H5 (raw UPDATE) means run status changes bypass the RunService state machine, risking data inconsistency. Both affect real user experience but are not security issues.
3. **Loose dependency pinning (low impact, medium likelihood).** 26 dependencies use `>=` constraints. A PyPI update could introduce breaking changes or vulnerabilities without warning. Not urgent but should be tightened before any production deployment.
