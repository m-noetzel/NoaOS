## Greatest Risk
Push notifications are structurally wired but functionally inert. The hooks in approval.py and runs/service.py call `should_notify()` and log "Push notification queued," but never call `apns.send()`. The APNsService's `_http_client` is never initialized. If the iOS client ships expecting push notifications, users will receive zero notifications despite the backend logging that it "queued" them. This is the project's recurring "wired in class, not called in use" pattern at a new level -- the wiring IS done, but the last method call is missing.
