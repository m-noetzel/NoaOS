## Risks You Are Taking
1. **iOS composition never tested (high impact, certain).** 10+ actors and 4+ ViewModels have 139+ unit tests with mocked dependencies but zero integration tests. The app target does not exist. Discovering a MainActor conflict or actor deadlock after shipping would require reworking multiple phases. This risk was flagged 3 health briefs ago and has not been addressed.
2. **Certificate pinning not wired (medium impact, certain).** CertificatePinningDelegate exists but is not connected to any URLSession. All iOS network traffic is unprotected against MITM until wiring is completed. This was deferred from iOS10 to iOS11 and is still deferred.
3. **Approval flow is entirely stub (medium impact, moderate).** Both pending (returns []) and decide (hardcoded response) endpoints are stubs. No DB persistence, no real approval workflow. iOS approval UI exercises only stub responses.
