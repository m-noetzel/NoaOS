## Greatest Risk
The iOS networking layer is a library with no app target. The APIClient, SSEClient, and all models compile and pass tests in isolation, but have never been instantiated inside a running iOS application. This mirrors the project's historical pattern (audit RC1: "Tests validated shape, not behavior"). The mitigating factor is that the backend contract tests ensure model compatibility, and the SPM package will be imported by the app target in iOS4/iOS5. But until iOS5 wires SSE streaming into a real chat view, the SSEClient reconnection logic (untested) and the actor-based APIClient (tested only via MockURLProtocol) have no proof of correct behavior in a real app context.
