## Greatest Risk
iOS5 has two confirmed contract mismatches between ChatViewModel and the backend -- result_ready reads `payload["text"]` when the backend sends `payload["response"]`, and classification_done reads `payload["domain"]` when the backend sends `payload["privacy_mode"]`. These bugs went undetected because zero Swift tests were written for iOS5. The result_ready mismatch means the final canonical response text will never replace accumulated tokens, leaving users with potentially incomplete or corrupted text. Combined with `try?` silently dropping decode failures, errors are invisible. This is a direct recurrence of audit finding RC1 ("tests validated shape, not behavior"). If this pattern continues into iOS6-iOS11, the entire iOS client will be a working build with silent runtime failures.

## Decisions Needed
- Fix the two contract mismatches and add Swift tests before marking iOS5 complete. The FAIL verdict blocks wave completion.
- The 14 pre-existing backend test failures have persisted across 5+ waves. Either schedule a dedicated fix phase or formally accept them as known debt.

## Risks You Are Taking
1. **Contract mismatches in iOS client (high impact, certain):** Two confirmed field name mismatches mean result_ready finalization and classification indicators silently fail. Users see incomplete token-accumulated text instead of the canonical response. Fix before shipping.
2. **14 pre-existing backend test failures (low impact, certain):** Persistent across 5+ waves. Reduces confidence in regression detection. These should either be fixed or formally marked as accepted technical debt.
